# Rules for agents in this workspace

- Work only inside this folder. Do not read or write outside it.
- An app is a folder with `aether.json` (kind `app`). Before changing one, read its `CLAUDE.md` and follow it.
- Change the data an app shows (e.g. `data.json`), not its `index.html`, unless the person asks for the page itself.
- Do not send anything to the outside (mail, posts, payments) on your own: say what you would send and stop.
- End with one or two lines: what changed, and which file.
